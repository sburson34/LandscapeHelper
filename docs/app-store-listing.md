## Full Description (Both Stores)

Turn your phone into a landscape designer. Landscape Helper analyzes photos of your yard and house exterior with AI and returns shrubbery picks, planting plans, and seasonal yard-work guides tailored to your local climate.

**How it works:**
1. Take photos of your yard, beds, or house exterior
2. Tell us your budget, vision, and ZIP code (type or use your voice)
3. Get a complete plan: plant picks, layout suggestions, step-by-step tasks, and seasonal timing

**Whole House Advice**
Photograph the front, sides, and back of your home — Landscape Helper looks at the whole property and recommends curb-appeal upgrades, shade trees, beds, and ground cover that work together as one design.

**Shrubbery Helper**
Snap any spot in your yard. The AI picks shrubs that fit your sun, soil, and ZIP-code climate, with notes on mature size, water needs, and bloom time so you don't over-plant or pick something that won't survive your winter.

**Region-Aware Weather**
We use your ZIP code (no GPS required) to pull local frost dates and upcoming forecasts, so the advice tells you the *right time* to plant — not just what to plant.

**Saved Projects & Honey-Do Lists**
- Save advice sessions to your account and revisit them later at landscape.diyhelper.org/account
- Track yard projects across the season
- Build shopping lists for the garden center and check items off as you buy them

**Know When to DIY and When to Call a Pro**
- Quote-request workflow for tree removal, irrigation, hardscape, and other jobs that need a licensed contractor
- Diagnose-mode for sick plants and lawn problems before they spread

**More Features:**
- Photo annotation to mark trouble spots
- Export landscape plans as PDF
- Multi-language support powered by on-device translation
- Dark mode

Whether it's a single bed or a full front-yard redesign, Landscape Helper gives you the plan and the confidence to plant it.
