Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-17

LandscapeHelper ("we", "the app") helps you plan landscaping and yard work with AI-generated advice tailored to the photos, descriptions, and local weather you share with it. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Account email address. Required to create an account, to let you sign back into your saved Whole House Advice sessions, and to verify a data-deletion request. Linked to your account; never sold or shared with advertisers.
  • Yard and house photos. When you ask for advice we send the photo(s) to our backend and on to the AI provider so it can analyze what's in them. Photos are processed in-flight and are not retained on our servers after the response is returned, with the limited exception of crash and abuse-investigation logs that may briefly include request metadata (never the photo bytes).
  • Free-form text you write describing your project. Treated the same as photos above — passed to the AI for the request, not retained.
  • Coarse location / ZIP code. Optional. Used to fetch local weather so seasonal advice is accurate. Stored as a preference, not linked to advertising identifiers, never used for tracking.
  • Weather data we fetch on your behalf. Derived from the ZIP code above; not personal data.
  • Crash and performance telemetry via Sentry. Stack traces and breadcrumbs only — no photo content, no user content. See app/src/services/sentry.js for the scrubbing rules.

What we do not collect

  • Precise GPS location.
  • Contacts, microphone, or device sensors.
  • Advertising identifiers; we run no ad SDKs and do no cross-app tracking.

Who processes your data

  • Our backend hosted on AWS (us-east-1).
  • OpenAI / Anthropic for AI advice generation. Photos and prompt text are sent over TLS; their privacy terms apply to the inflight request.
  • A weather provider for ZIP-code-based forecasts.
  • Sentry for crash reporting.

Data retention

Photos and prompt text: not retained after the response.
Account email and saved advice history: until you delete your account.
Crash telemetry: 90 days.

Deleting your data

In-app: Settings → Delete Account. The app calls POST /api/account/delete; we email a verification link, and once you confirm we remove your account and saved data within 30 days.

By email: bursons@gmail.com with subject "LandscapeHelper deletion".

Children

LandscapeHelper is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security disclosure

Report vulnerabilities to bursons@gmail.com or see /.well-known/security.txt.

Changes

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes materially. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
